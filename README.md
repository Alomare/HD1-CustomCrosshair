# Custom Crosshair

A crosshair mod for HELLDIVERS (2015), for the [Obsidian](https://github.com/Alomare/ObsidianLauncher) mod manager.

Custom Crosshair replaces the aim cursor with a crosshair built from shapes, with one look between shots and another while aiming. Shape, size, gap, color, outline and shadow are all settings. A hitmarker flashes around the crosshair when a shot from this player hurts or kills an enemy.

## How to use

The settings are in the Options tab of Custom Crosshair in Obsidian, with a live preview. A change applies at the next launch of the game.

With Integrated Third Person, the crosshair goes where shots land. Turn off **Third person crosshair** to keep the crosshair of that mod instead.

## Known issues

- On a controller the game shows no cursor in the top-down view, so no crosshair shows there.
- A shot that armor stops does not count as a hit.
