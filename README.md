# Custom Crosshair

A crosshair mod for HELLDIVERS (2015), for the [Obsidian](https://github.com/Alomare/ObsidianLauncher) mod manager.

Custom Crosshair replaces the aim cursor with a crosshair made of shapes, with separate looks for idle and aiming. Shape, size, gap, color, outline and shadow are all settings. A hitmarker flashes around the crosshair, with a sound, when your shots hurt or kill enemies.

## How to use

Settings are in the Options tab of Custom Crosshair in Obsidian, with a live preview. Changes apply at the next game launch.

With Eyes of Liberty, the crosshair shows where shots land. Turn off **Third person crosshair** to keep the game crosshair there instead. Turn off **First person crosshair** to hide the crosshair in the first person camera of the Eyes of Liberty beta.

## Known issues

- On controllers the game shows no cursor in the top-down view, so no crosshair shows there either.
- Shots stopped by armor don't count as hits.
